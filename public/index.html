Waitlist status guide

GNWL
General waitlist — the most common type, clears first as confirmed passengers cancel.
RAC
Reservation Against Cancellation — you already share a berth and move to a full one as seats free up.
PQWL
Pooled Quota waitlist — reserved for a specific stretch of the journey between two stations.
TQWL
Tatkal waitlist — from last-minute Tatkal quota bookings, usually clears slower than GNWL.
